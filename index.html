Questions riders ask us

Frequently asked questions

Do you come to the customer's location?
Yes. Marc Cycling is a fully mobile service: the mechanic comes to your home, office or building with professional tools, and most repairs are done on the spot. There is no shop to visit and no pickup fee.
Which areas do you serve?
Broward County and South Florida, including Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Pompano Beach, Coral Springs, Davie, Plantation, Sunrise, Weston, Miramar, Coconut Creek and Miami. Not sure about your city? Call (754) 252-5245 and we'll tell you right away.
Do you work on e-bikes?
Yes. We service e-bikes mechanically: motor and system diagnostics, battery checks, brakes, gears, tires and full tune-ups (E-Bike Silver from $150, E-Bike Gold from $220). We do not rebuild batteries or modify motors.
How much does a mobile bike tune-up cost?
Tune-up packages start at $95: Bronze from $95, Silver from $140 and Gold from $215. For e-bikes, Silver is from $150 and Gold from $220. A standard flat tire repair is $49 plus tube. Prices are for labor only, parts not included. Prices updated July 2026.
How do I schedule an appointment?
Book online at marccycling.com/book, with Saturday and Sunday appointments from 7am to 5pm Eastern. You can also call or WhatsApp (754) 252-5245 any day of the week, we usually reply within minutes.
Is there a service-call or pickup fee?
No. The price is the price: no pickup fees and no hidden fees. Estimates by phone are free, and if your bike doesn't need something on the list, we tell you on the spot.
Do you provide the parts?
Prices cover labor only; parts are charged separately. Common parts like inner tubes are installed on-site (tubes run $24 to $40 depending on size).
What payment methods do you accept?
Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay, checks and cash. Credit cards are not accepted.
